Executive Summary & Strategic Scope

Bangladesh faces recurring severe hydro-meteorological shocks—including monsoonal flash floods across the Surma-Meghna-Jamuna basins, Bay of Bengal cyclone storm surges, and localized waterlogging. While the Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP) and Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) provide macroscopic forecasting, a critical operational gap exists between early forecast issuance, localized sensor verification, and automated last-mile disaster relief dispatch.

GreenSignal AI establishes a unified physical and digital infrastructure connecting over 2.5 million grassroots field workers, 5,000+ Upazila and Union coordinators, and national ministry executives. The initiative combines autonomous real-time telemetry, predictive hydrological modeling, multi-agency communication pipelines, and localized buffer storage to ensure communities can withstand shocks and resume normal economic life rapidly.

Multi-Tier Role Applications

Tailored client experiences for Field Responders (low-bandwidth/offline-first PWA & mobile app), Upazila/District Command Coordinators (tactical GIS and relief allocation suite), and Ministry Personnel (macro analytics, resource rebalancing, and national siren broadcast).

Physical Infrastructure

495 Upazila Disaster Operations Hubs and 1,250 climate-hardened Union Relief Buffer Warehouses and Shelters with solar-powered cold-chain medical storage and grain preservation.

Automated Environmental Sensor Network

2,500+ industrial IoT telemetry stations across river basins, coastal surge points, and sluices providing 24/7 autonomous ground truth without relying on human observer presence during severe weather.

Integration & Modernization

Ingests existing telemetry from BMD Doppler radars, FFWC hydrologic gauges, and SPARRSO satellite data, modernizing manual relief distribution into biometric and QR-code verified workflows.

Software Architecture & Live Codebase Walkthrough

The GreenSignal AI digital platform is built on an enterprise-grade TypeScript/Node.js microservices framework paired with PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, and Socket.io real-time event streaming:

CLIENT LAYER PWA / Android

Field Worker Application

  • Offline-first execution utilizing client-side caching (IndexedDB/Service Workers) ensuring full operational continuity under intermittent 2G/EDGE connectivity.
  • GPS-tagged emergency distress reporting and survivor evacuation routing.
  • QR-code verified relief handoffs eliminating duplicate ration distribution.
COMMAND SUITE public/platform.html

Coordinator Tactical Suite & GIS

  • Interactive Leaflet GIS mapping rendering 576+ nationwide upazila disaster nodes, 503+ relief shelters, and live water-level telemetry.
  • Relief allocation optimization engine calculating food grain, potable water, medical supplies, and temporary shelter kits against real-time population density.
  • District-level bounding zoom and automated resource shortfall flagging.
REAL-TIME LAYER src/services/messaging.ts

Privacy-Isolated Real-Time Messenger

  • Channel-isolated Socket.io event architecture separating public broadcast rooms (upazila-general) from end-to-end direct conversations (direct-userIdA-userIdB).
  • Debounced directory search across all system responders (by username, full name, role, organization, and phone number).
  • Strict access control preventing data leaks across accounts.
DATA LAYER prisma/schema.prisma

Relational Data Model & PostgreSQL

  • Normalized schemas managing DistrictNode, User, ReliefCenter, EnvironmentalSensor, DisasterAlert, ShelterAllocation, and ChatMessage.
  • Foreign key cascades and indexes enabling sub-millisecond lookups under crisis traffic.

Network Infrastructure for Multi-Million User Scale

To guarantee 99.999% uptime during Category 4/5 cyclone landfalls and national flood crises:

Tier-IV National Data Center Hosting

Primary deployment in the Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC) Tier-IV National Data Center (Kaliakoir, Gazipur) with active-active hot failover and isolated power redundancy.

Global Edge & BDIX Anycast Routing

Edge layer terminating SSL, filtering DDoS attacks (up to 500 Gbps), and serving static web assets directly through the Bangladesh Internet Exchange (BDIX) peering fabric for sub-15ms domestic latency.

Kubernetes Autoscaling & In-Memory Cache Grid

Microservice pods dynamically scale from 32 to 512+ instances via Horizontal Pod Autoscalers (HPA). High-throughput Redis Enterprise Cluster acts as the Socket.io pub/sub adapter, sustaining 1.5 million concurrent persistent WebSocket connections.

Time-Series Telemetry Ingestion (TimescaleDB & PgBouncer)

Partitioned PostgreSQL hyper-tables ingesting millions of telemetry readings per minute with 7-day automated rollups.

Telecom Integration & Satellite Fallback

Dedicated private APN (Access Point Name) with zero-rated responder data across Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, and Teletalk. High-risk coastal islands (e.g., Char Fasson, Sandwip, Hatiya) equipped with Bangabandhu Satellite-1 (BS-1) VSAT backup links and solar LoRaWAN gateway towers (15 km line-of-sight range). Multi-operator SMPP gateways delivering up to 100,000 Cell Broadcast alerts per second.

Implementation Roadmap (36 Months Phased Rollout)

PHASE 1 (MONTHS 01–06) Target Pilot: Sunamganj, Kurigram, Barguna

Foundation, Cloud Core & 25-Upazila Pilot

Tier-IV cluster setup at BCC Gazipur, telco private APN integration, and 25-Upazila pilot across high-vulnerability hotspots: Sunamganj (flash floods), Kurigram (riverine floods), and Barguna (coastal surge). Initial deployment of 300 IoT sensors.

PHASE 2 (MONTHS 07–20) Target Scope: 250 Upazilas & 600 Warehouses

Regional Expansion & Volunteer Onboarding

Retrofit of 250 Upazila Disaster Hubs, construction of 600 Union buffer warehouses, deployment of 1,500 IoT sensor stations, and mobile app enrollment of 1,000,000 grassroots volunteers.

PHASE 3 (MONTHS 21–36) Target Scope: Complete Nationwide Coverage

Nationwide Saturation & Inter-Agency Drills

Nationwide saturation across remaining 245 Upazilas and 650 Union centers, AI predictive model integration with BMD/FFWC, satellite VSAT terminal activation, and joint mock drills with the Armed Forces Division (AFD) and CPP.
